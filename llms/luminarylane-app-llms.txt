# Luminary Lane

> Luminary Lane is an agentic AI platform featuring Lane, a Chief Marketing AI-Agent who works with your team to find, test, and scale the marketing mix that creates traction.

## Overview

Luminary Lane provides Lane, a Chief Marketing AI-Agent for startups and SMEs working to find marketing channels that prove traction. Lane works through the web platform and can collaborate through Slack, Microsoft Teams, and WhatsApp. She coordinates 19 specialized marketing channel agents while keeping people in control of consequential work. Luminary Lane is also exploring account-focused marketing workflows with enterprise revenue teams.

Lane helps teams:
- Build a brand foundation and traction goal from their business context
- Map the relevant marketing channels, choose bounded tests, and rule out weak bets early
- Generate and coordinate approved work across content, search, paid acquisition, email, partnerships, events, and other channels
- Keep human review and approval at the gate before agreed actions are put into motion
- Measure evidence from each test and update the next version of the GTM motion
